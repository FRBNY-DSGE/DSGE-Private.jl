PRIORS USED
•	η : https://www.dallasfed.org/~/media/documents/institute/wpapers/2019/0359.pdf
•	ω:  https://www.dallasfed.org/~/media/documents/institute/wpapers/2019/0359.pdf
•	ξ_p : ζ_p in SmetsWouters
•	γ_r: ρ in SmetsWouters
•	γ_π: ψ1 in AnSchorfheide
•	ρ_m : exogenous processes - autocorrelation in SmetsWouters / https://www.dallasfed.org/~/media/documents/institute/wpapers/2019/0359.pdf
•	σ_m : σ_R in AnSchorfheide
•	ρ_z : https://www.dallasfed.org/~/media/documents/institute/wpapers/2019/0359.pdf
•	σ_z : σ_z in AnSchorfheide
•	ρ_uip : exogenous processes - autocorrelation in SmetsWouters
•	σ_uip : σ_z in AnSchorfheide
